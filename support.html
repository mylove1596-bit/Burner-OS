Support

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Burner OS is an offline narrative detective game for iPhone. This page covers the questions we are asked most often, and how to reach us.

Requirements

  • iPhone on iOS 17.0 or later
  • About 60 MB of free storage
  • No account, no sign-in, and no internet connection — the game is fully offline

Getting started

The game opens on the Cold Case Archive, a list of eleven case files.

  • Case #01 — The Marina Vanishing is free and fully playable from the start.
  • Every other case shows a LOCKED badge until you buy a clearance pass.
  • Open a case to read the Case Briefing, then work the seized phone: Messages, Ledger, Signal Log, SecVault and Warrant Terminal.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I open the other ten cases?

The free tier lets you try the game before you buy: 1 hint per case, 1 warrant submission per case, and 3 PIN guesses per case. Case #01 has no limits at all. Buying a pass removes every limit.

A case locked me out after three wrong PINs.

That is by design — each case hides a four-digit vault PIN and three wrong guesses end the attempt for that case. If you have a pass, the PIN guesses are unlimited and you can keep trying.

I paid but the cases are still locked.

Your entitlement is restored automatically from the App Store receipt on the same Apple Account. If you have just installed the app, or switched devices, use Restore Purchases at the bottom of the clearance paywall. If it still does not work, make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase, and then get in touch.

What do the passes cost?

The clearance paywall shows three tiers side by side, with the price for your region taken live from the App Store:

  • Weekly Investigator Pass — 7 days of full access
  • Annual Detective Pass — 12 months of full access
  • Lifetime Clearance Archive — one payment, permanent access

The app never hard-codes a price; it always displays the App Store's own price for your account and region.

Does the game need to be online?

No. The entire game runs offline. There is no account, no server and no tracking.

Where is my progress stored?

On your device only. Uninstalling the app removes it, so there is nothing to sync. The app contains a Restore Purchases button so your paid access can be re-applied on a new device.

Will my purchases work on a new iPhone?

Yes, as long as you sign in to the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases.

The game is in English only. Will there be other languages?

The game interface is English. The App Store listing is provided in English (US) and Spanish (Mexico).

Does the app contain ads or track me?

No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no account. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a problem

If something is broken, wrong or unreadable, please email us with:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version
  • the case number and what you were doing
  • a screenshot, if it helps
