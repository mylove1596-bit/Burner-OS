Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The short version: Burner OS collects nothing. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Burner OS is published by an independent developer. Questions about this policy can be sent to mylove1596@gamail.com.

2. What we collect

Nothing.

Burner OS has no server of its own. There is no sign-up, no email address, no name, no advertising identifier (IDFA), and no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. The app does not ask for the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it does not track you across other apps or websites.

3. What stays on your device

The following is stored locally on your device, and is never transmitted anywhere:

DataWhy
Which cases you have opened, solved or closedTo show your archive and your progress
Your remaining hint, warrant and PIN-guess allowances per caseTo limit the free tier to one hint, one warrant submission and three PIN guesses per case
Your unlock status for the paid tiersTo show which cases are available to you
Your settings, such as haptics and audioYour gameplay preferences

4. The app is offline by design

Burner OS ships with all eleven cases inside the app bundle and reads them from local storage. No case data, no progress and no telemetry is requested from or sent to a server, because there is no server. The "Messages" screen inside the game shows pre-written fictional case evidence — it is not a messaging feature, it sends nothing, and it does not read your own Messages app.

5. Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your card details, your billing address or your Apple ID. Apple may share limited transaction information with us (for example, a purchase token and a subscription status) so that we can unlock the cases you paid for. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your device's Settings.

The app stores the App Store receipt on your device so it can offer you a Restore Purchases button. That receipt is never uploaded to us.

6. Children

Burner OS is rated 16+ and is not directed at children. It contains fictional crime fiction, including depictions of violence, that is intended for adult readers. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, at any age, so there is no information to delete on request.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information on our servers, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete. To remove everything the app holds, delete the app from your device — this clears its local storage along with it.

8. Third parties

Burner OS uses no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, no crash-reporting services and no social logins. The only third party involved is Apple, which delivers the app and handles payments.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date at the top. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
