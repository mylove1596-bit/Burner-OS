# Privacy Policy — Burner: Cold Case OS

**Last updated: 14 November 2026**

Burner: Cold Case OS is an offline narrative detective game for iPhone. This policy explains
what the app does with data — the short answer is: it does not collect any.

## Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| Data collected | **None** |
| Data used to track you | **None** |
| Ads or analytics | **None** |
| Third-party SDKs | **None** |
| Account required | **No** |

## Data we do not collect

The app does **not** collect, transmit, sell, share or disclose any personal information.
Specifically, we do not collect:

- Name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information
- Accounts, usernames, passwords, or sign-in credentials
- Photos, contacts, location, calendar, or health information
- Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or IP addresses
- Usage analytics, crash analytics, session recordings, or telemetry
- Diagnostic or crash reports
- Purchase history (see [Purchases](#purchases) below)

## How the app works

Burner: Cold Case OS is entirely offline. It communicates with no server, because it has no
server.

- All case text, evidence, and game data ship inside the app bundle and are read from local storage on your device.
- Your progress (which cases you have opened, which cases you have solved) is stored **only on your device** and never leaves it.
- The app has no sign-in screen, no login, no user ID, and no server-side profile.
- The app contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, attribution frameworks, or crash-reporting services.
- The "Messages" screen inside the game shows pre-written fictional case evidence. It is not a messaging feature, it does not send anything, and it does not access your Messages app.

## Purchases

Paid access is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.

- Purchases are completed in Apple's standard payment sheet, so your payment details are handled by Apple, never by us.
- We receive no payment details, no card numbers, and no purchase amounts tied to your identity.
- Apple may provide us with aggregate, non-identifying sales statistics under its standard developer agreement.
- Your entitlement (whether you have access to the paid tiers) is determined on your device from the App Store receipt issued to your Apple Account.
- The app contains a **Restore Purchases** button so you can re-apply a previous purchase on a new device signed in to the same Apple Account.

## Local storage

The app stores your progress, settings, and App Store receipt on your device so the game works
offline. This data stays on your device. Deleting the app removes it. We cannot see it and
cannot retrieve it for you.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and is rated 16+. It collects no data from anyone,
including children.

## Third parties

The app links out to Apple-controlled pages (the standard Apple End-User License Agreement and
the App Store). Tapping such a link opens Apple's website or the App Store, governed by
Apple's own privacy policy. We do not receive information from those pages.

## Your rights

Because we collect no data, there is no personal data for us to access, export, correct, or
delete on request. If you uninstall the app, all local data is removed from your device by
iOS.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be committed to this repository and the
"Last updated" date above will change. The version published here is the version that
applies.
